# Project 4: Data Visualization

## Objective

The objective of this project is to create meaningful and visually appealing charts that effectively communicate insights from an e-commerce dataset. Data visualization helps transform raw data into understandable graphical representations, making it easier to identify trends, patterns, and business insights.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Google Colab
* Pandas
* Matplotlib

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains e-commerce transaction records, including product information, customer orders, payment methods, order status, quantities, prices, and transaction dates.

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Product-wise Sales (Bar Chart)

A bar chart was created to compare the total sales generated by each product.

**Insight:**

* Printer and Chair generated the highest sales.
* Laptop and Tablet also contributed significantly to overall revenue.
* Phone recorded the lowest sales among all products.

---

### 2. Order Status Distribution (Pie Chart)

A pie chart was used to display the percentage distribution of different order statuses.

**Insight:**

* Orders are fairly evenly distributed across all statuses.
* Cancelled orders have the highest percentage.
* Delivered orders represent the lowest percentage among the order statuses.

---

### 3. Payment Method Usage (Bar Chart)

A bar chart was created to analyze customer payment preferences.

**Insight:**

* Online payment is the most commonly used payment method.
* Cash is the second most preferred payment option.
* Credit Card, Debit Card, and Gift Card have similar usage frequencies.

---

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)

A line chart was created to visualize changes in total sales over time.

**Insight:**

* Monthly sales show variations throughout the year.
* The chart helps identify periods of higher and lower sales activity, enabling trend analysis.

---

### 5. Quantity Distribution (Histogram)

A histogram was created to analyze the frequency distribution of product quantities ordered.

**Insight:**

* Most customers purchase a moderate number of items per transaction.
* Extremely large order quantities are less common.

---

### 6. Total Price Distribution (Box Plot)

A box plot was generated to identify the spread of transaction values and detect outliers.

**Insight:**

* Most transactions fall within the normal price range.
* A few high-value transactions appear as outliers, indicating expensive purchases.
* No abnormal data inconsistencies were observed.

## Key Insights

* Product sales differ significantly, with Printer and Chair generating the highest revenue.
* Online payments are the preferred payment method among customers.
* Order statuses are balanced, indicating a consistent order management process.
* Sales trends fluctuate over time, highlighting seasonal or periodic changes.
* Most customers place orders with moderate quantities.
* High-value transactions exist but represent a small portion of the dataset.

## Conclusion

Data visualization transformed the raw dataset into meaningful graphical insights, making it easier to understand customer behavior, sales performance, payment preferences, and transaction patterns. The visualizations clearly highlight important business trends and support data-driven decision-making. This project demonstrates the effective use of Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib to create professional and informative visual representations of data.
